HOW TO PLAY

WASD / ARROWS Steer, Accelerate, Brake/Reverse
SPACEBAR Drift (hold while turning for tighter cornering)
C KEY Change Camera (5 Views including Top-Down)
+/- or SCROLL Zoom Camera (Top-Down view only)
R KEY Reset car to track if stuck
M KEY Mute audio
ESC KEY Pause game
DRS: Activates automatically when within 1 second of the car ahead in fast zones.
SLIPSTREAM: Gain extra speed when following closely behind another car.
