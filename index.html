HOW TO PLAY

    
WASD / ARROWS Precise Steering, Accelerate, Brake/Reverse

    
MOBILE TILT / GYRO Tilt device Left/Right to turn wheels sharply. Tilt Forward for Gas, Backward to slow down. Dedicated BRAKE & DRIFT triggers.

    
GYRO SENSITIVITY Choose Low, Med, High, or Ultra for immediate sharp steering lock.

    
SPACE / DRIFT Hold during cornering to initiate power slide & tight apex turn.

    
C KEY / CAM CHIP Cycle 5 Camera Views (Chase, Cockpit/Hood, Trackside TV, Orbit, Top-Down).

    
+/- OR SCROLL Zoom Top-Down camera altitude.

    
R KEY Reset car to track centerline if stuck or spun out.

    
⛶ FULLSCREEN Expand to immersive full-screen display.

    
DRS ZONES: Opens rear wing for +18 km/h top speed when within 1.2s of the car ahead.

    
SLIPSTREAM: Draft closely behind rivals down long straights for maximum acceleration.
